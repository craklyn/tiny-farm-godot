    Fix harvest crash and center-point collision overlap
    
    - farm.gd: add missing get_crop_type(), whose absence crashed every harvest
      (script error at player.gd:363) and broke the harvest->ship->gold loop
    - player.gd: probe the player's leading edge (COLLIDE_RADIUS) for tile
      collision instead of the center, so the sprite no longer sinks halfway
      into obstacles before stopping
    
    Both headless suites now pass: tools/test_runner.tscn 20/20,
    tests/test_runner.gd 117/117.
