- (2026-09-03) The CEO set studio writing rules, canonical in `docs/WRITING.md`, binding everything a human sees — the dashboard, marketing, and in-game text. Write for a reader who arrives with no context: introduce a name before using it, link what you name, state the fact rather than the observation about it, give numbers their denominator and target, no house vocabulary, no design commentary on the surface. Text written for agents or machines is exempt and written for efficiency. Translations preserve the register of their source: in-game text stays minimal, marketing stays plain, and a translation that adds words the source avoided breaks the wordless constraint where it applies (S-7, phase 1's farming loop).
