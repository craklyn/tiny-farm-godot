- (2026-09-02) Daniel's standing aesthetic rule for the presentation layer, given after the cold open's neighbour was seen watering in silence: **when an actor takes an action and it is in the player's focus, that action gets the same treatment of visualization and sound the player's own action gets.** The cue belongs to the verb, not to whoever performed it — a neighbour, a replayed session, a phase-4 bot doing the chore all look and sound like a pair of hands doing that verb. Silence for a non-player actor is a bug to fix, not a scope line to defend; do not ship a verb-by-verb subset again.
- (2026-09-02) The one limit on that rule is the player's attention, not the actor's nature. Bulk or off-screen work does not get the full per-action treatment: a sprinkler waters nine tiles inside a single day turn and answers with one spray animation rather than nine pours (those Actions resolve inside advance_day and never reach the cue table), and a farm nobody is playing stays muted. Implementation: world/farm.gd ACTOR_VERB_CUES, filled from player.gd's own answers verb for verb, filtering the player out so her cues are not doubled.
