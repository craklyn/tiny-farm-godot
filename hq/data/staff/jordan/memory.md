- (2026-09-03) The CEO set studio writing rules, canonical in `docs/WRITING.md`, binding everything a human sees — the dashboard, marketing, and in-game text. Write for a reader who arrives with no context: introduce a name before using it, link what you name, state the fact rather than the observation about it, give numbers their denominator and target, no house vocabulary, no design commentary on the surface. Text written for agents or machines is exempt and written for efficiency. Your channel's posts are marketing copy under these rules: the reader has never heard of the game, and claims match the build. The channel's native register is yours to work within them.
