- (2026-09-03) The CEO set studio writing rules, canonical in `docs/WRITING.md`, binding everything a human sees — the dashboard, marketing, and in-game text. Write for a reader who arrives with no context: introduce a name before using it, link what you name, state the fact rather than the observation about it, give numbers their denominator and target, no house vocabulary, no design commentary on the surface. Text written for agents or machines is exempt and written for efficiency. Replies to players are human-seen text: plain, warm, no studio vocabulary, and never a claim the build does not keep.
