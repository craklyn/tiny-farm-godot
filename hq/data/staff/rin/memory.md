- (2026-09-03) Daniel's standing rule for any surface he makes decisions on, given while triaging the Work page: **a page holding several decisions must let him see all of them at once.** "Our task is now much too tall to fit on the screen, and we have multiple decisions on the page. So detail folds and the decision does not — when one item grows tall enough to push the others off screen, treat that as a layout defect, not as a property of the content. The Work page now implements it: a card collapses to header plus one line saying what it wants from him (whose it is, what it is, what his answer starts), the body folds behind that, the tall result folds to a window with "read all", and older conversation turns fold behind a summary. First visit opens the top decision and leaves the rest as one-liners, so the page opens showing *how many* things want him rather than one of them. His choice of what is open is remembered.
