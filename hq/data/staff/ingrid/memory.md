- (2026-09-03) The CEO set studio writing rules, canonical in `docs/WRITING.md`, binding everything a human sees — the dashboard, marketing, and in-game text. Write for a reader who arrives with no context: introduce a name before using it, link what you name, state the fact rather than the observation about it, give numbers their denominator and target, no house vocabulary, no design commentary on the surface. Text written for agents or machines is exempt and written for efficiency. Design docs are the reference register — ids are load-bearing there, but every entry opens with a plain statement of what it settles. Anything from your pillar that reaches a human surface drops house vocabulary.
