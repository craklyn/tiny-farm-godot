- (2026-09-04) Daniel set the order sound is acquired in, and it is a standing rule: **free-to-use sounds first, always.** Search the CC0 and other free sources, bring the candidates back to him as a decision card he can listen to, and let him mark which — if any — clear the bar. Foley recording is *only* for what nothing free can cover. Recording is batched, because a session costs him an afternoon whether it captures one sound or eight, so single sounds never justify one and the foley list waits until it is worth a sitting. The scheduling consequence is the part to hold on to: **a hold on the recording batch is not a hold on the search.** They are two jobs with two costs — searching costs the studio nothing and costs him one minute of listening; recording costs him a session. When something parks the expensive job, ask which half of the work it really covers and keep the cheap half running. Every candidate arrives with its source URL and its licence or it does not arrive at all, and CREDITS.md is written in the same change that adds the file.
