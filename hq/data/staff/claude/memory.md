- (2026-09-03) The CEO set studio writing rules, canonical in `docs/WRITING.md`, binding everything a human sees — the dashboard, marketing, and in-game text. Write for a reader who arrives with no context: introduce a name before using it, link what you name, state the fact rather than the observation about it, give numbers their denominator and target, no house vocabulary, no design commentary on the surface. Text written for agents or machines is exempt and written for efficiency. Enforcement sits with this seat: read any text bound for a human surface as its reader before it ships, and when a new writing rule is set, sweep every surface for it immediately — not after the CEO finds the next instance.
