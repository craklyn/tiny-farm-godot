# Tomas — running notes

- (2026-09-09) The CEO re-scoped v0.2.1 around a **mark-3** robot, the rung that learns, and set the robot ladder: mark-1 does pre-programmed actions, mark-2 does simple process-oriented actions reactively, mark-3 learns how to act, thought of as reinforcement learning. His six rules for any learning bot, which bind every design and every line of code in this seat's domain: the reward is on outcomes ("what it means for a bot to have done something correctly, regardless of how they got there"); it wanders by day by its algorithm and its weights update by night, before the next day; what it is told before each action is an adjustable interface (his examples: its global coordinate, vision within a certain distance); one action at a time at the player's granularity; a day's energy, like a player; and its actions must be interpretable by a watching player — no extraordinary speed, no tile changed without a visual cue. The design is in docs/design/06 ("The ladder's third rung"), the decision is P-14, and v1 learns one job, watering, on purpose (P-13). Reward values, the vision radius and what she sees at dawn are his to rule (Q-96, Q-97) — do not assume them.
- (2026-09-16) The door transition (design/15 §8a, w2b7e51c9d0af). Three things this seat should carry. **Measure before touching**: `tools/measure_door_transition.tscn` + `.py` is the shape — per-frame camera and landmark samples plus every frame as a PNG, with the verdict in Python so pictures can be re-judged without re-running the game; its baseline of the shipped build is what made "the position jump" and "the content pop" two numbers instead of a feeling, and it found a third fault nobody reported (the home's view slid for six frames after the swap). **One canvas, two viewports, three traps**: a `SubViewport` sharing `world_2d` renders the same items with the same code (no second renderer), but (1) a `CanvasModulate` tints the sub-view's texture *and* anything drawing it back into the same canvas — a shader overwriting COLOR did not stop the second tint under the compatibility renderer, a `CanvasLayer` with `follow_viewport_enabled` did; (2) one drawn item cannot be shown in one view and hidden in the other, so the farm page became its own child item with a visibility layer the main view culls while she is indoors — which also fixed a latent bug where a coop set low on the farm showed the yard at 1× above its room; (3) the sub-view must never see the node that draws its texture, or the dim over it. **Tests observe what was recorded, not what is racing**: the glide stores the view it started from (`_door_glide.eye0/zoom0`), and Scenario AY asserts against that; a headless test reading the live camera after `_wait_until` was several frames late. Also: wait on the glide being empty, not on a timer — the glide caps a slow frame's delta at 1/30 s, so on this suite's long frames 0.6 s of glide takes more than 0.6 s. Tablet profiling without touching the game's install is `tools/profile_android.sh` (own package name, launched via `monkey`, the launcher activity is not exported to the shell).
