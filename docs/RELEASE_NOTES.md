# Release notes

*What a player reads when an update ships. Newest first. Each release's section is
the text posted as the itch.io devlog for that tag — written for someone who arrives
with no context (`docs/WRITING.md`), so no internal names, no ticket IDs. The store
page copy lives in `ITCH_PAGE.md`; these are the per-update announcements.*

## v0.2.0 — Update 1 · 2026-09-08

The farm got its first machines — and a home.

- **A shop that sells everything.** Seeds and machines, all pictures, coins and
  numbers. No reading needed.
- **Robots you can teach.** Show the Mark I which squares to water and it does that
  job. Give it a stall to live in and it heads out each morning on its own, does its
  rounds, and walks back to its bay. The Mark II chases birds off, follows you, or
  patrols.
- **A home to go inside.** Your farmhouse opens — walk through the front door, and
  there's your bed.
- **The day feels real.** A clock you can read, soil that darkens when watered and
  dries in the sun, and at dusk your front door glows to show you the way to bed.
- Plus: real recorded water sounds, a wilder wild field beyond the yard, clearing
  costs that match what you're clearing, small pictures over each station saying
  what it's for, and a proper app icon.

## v0.1.0 · 2026-08-30

The first public build: a small farm you work with one finger — clear, till, plant,
water, sleep, harvest, sell — with a hen, a crow, and a day that ends in the yard.
