# 08 — Narrative (Story Bible)

*Status: skeleton. This chapter is `[Designer]`-led — it needs authored fiction, with
Claude drafting to the designer's direction. Needed by: M4 planning at the latest (D-3
trigger: waves need identity before the first tower is specced).*

## Questions the bible must answer (D-3)
1. **What are the pests?** Insects/animals/something stranger — and why do they raid?
   **Leading hypothesis (ruled 2026-08-18, held until something better):** the **pest
   queen who farms her own resources** — a P-9 verb-complete entity; waves come from
   somewhere that *grows* them; phase 5 marches on her farm. Mechanically nearly free;
   fictionally it mirrors the player (two farmers, one valley) and gives phase 5 its
   destination. Design against it; don't lock expensive content to it yet.
2. **World premise** — why is the player here, alone, with one yard? Who was here
   before? (A prior farm's ruins are cheap worldbuilding + tutorial props.)
3. **Tone gradient** — cozy start → epic end without whiplash; where the game sits for
   a 4-year-old at phase 1 (S-7 tone floor: nothing scary in the yard) vs. an adult at
   phase 5.
4. **The bots' fictional status** — machines? golems? seeds that grew legs? This choice
   colors phases 4–5 entirely and should harmonize with the training fantasy
   (mentorship, dreams, exams).
5. **Character roster** — is anyone else in the world? A merchant? The daughter-friendly
   layer likes at least one friendly face.
6. **Naming conventions** — crops, pests, towers, bot models; consistency rules.
7. **The ending** — what phase 5 resolves; whether the queen (if she exists) is
   defeated, displaced, or *negotiated with* (a farming game may prefer coexistence to
   extermination — designer's call on what the game believes).

## Working method
`[Designer]` rules on 1, 3, 4, 7 (the load-bearing fiction). `[Claude]` drafts full
bible text, names, and barks from those rulings for approval. Keep the bible short: one
page of truth beats ten pages of lore debt.
