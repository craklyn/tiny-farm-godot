# 07 — Expedition System

*Status: skeleton, deliberately thin (D-1: genre undecidable until phase-4 bots
demonstrably fight). This chapter exists to hold constraints, not designs.*

## What is already true
- Farm persists as home base; expeditions are excursion maps (P-3).
- The squad is the player + *their* trained bots — per-bot adapters are the party
  members' identities (P-5); expedition performance reflects how you raised them.
- Entry is diegetic: track pest trails backward to the nest (P-10).
- The 4→5 transition arrives as a natural capability crest (P-4 ruling).
- Squad orders travel the same message channel as bot pings (P-7) — command verbs are
  the tactical interface, whatever the genre.
- Emergent possibility, zero committed scope (ruled 2026-08-18): comms interception —
  enemies with audio perception can hear bot pings, and vice versa. Falls out of channel
  symmetry; decide whether to *use* it at D-1.

## Decided at D-1 (not before)
- Genre: turn-based tactics / real-time-with-pause / autobattler-with-orders / twitch
  hybrid — with P-1 premise 1's escape clauses on the table.
- Whether expeditions feed farming back (resources, new crops, captured pests as
  training data).
- Loss rules for bots (permadeath tension vs. the attachment economy).
- The ending: what "done" means for the whole game (with 08-narrative).
