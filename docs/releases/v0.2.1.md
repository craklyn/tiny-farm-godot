# Tiny Farm v0.2.1

The game has updated. Your existing farms will continue after the update.

## New: the Industrial Barn

Collect 10 eggs and a red dairy barn appears in the shop. It has cows, and a cheese factory inside.

- The barn costs 500 gold and comes with its first cow. Each extra cow costs 250 gold, with four places in each barn shared by cows and sheep. You can have two barns.
- Tap the barn and choose Go inside. The room is big: a pen for each of four cows on one side, and the cheese factory on the other. The cows walk in through the big doors by themselves, choose a pen and give milk. They are never shut in.
- Milk goes through seven steps, each on its own large steel machine, from the milk receiver to the press and the belt. Each one shows its work for five seconds, so you can follow the milk until it comes out as cheese.
- Finished cheese waits on a shelf at the end of the line. Walk up and tap the shelf once to sell everything on it, 20 gold for each batch.
- The cows are black and white, and each stands about as high as the farmer's shoulder.
- Between milkings a cow grazes in the field near the barn, and the herd spreads out across the grass. She walks round your beds and crops, and crosses them only when it is the one way back to the barn.

## New: sheep that get too fluffy to walk

Buy a sheep for 250 gold once you have a barn. Cows and sheep share its four places.
A sheep's wool grows each night until she is too fluffy to move, and she stays happily
where she stopped. There are no shears yet.

A stuck sheep grazes a bare patch around herself, stretches for the grass just beyond
it, and sometimes has a small bird perch on her fleece.

## Also new

- Meet the Mark III, a farm robot that learns as it works. It can help across the farm and practise overnight.
- Build a training workbench to see what your Mark III is learning, choose how it practises, and set its learning pace.
- Grow peas after your first wheat harvest.
- Keep up to three separate farms.
- Step inside your home and chicken coop, add a nest box or rug, and build a Spiral Tower with a view of your farm.
- Carry more crops, see how many of each seed you carry out of how many you can, choose seeds from picture buttons, and scroll the shop with a finger.
- Sprinklers now cost 50 gold. Buying your first fence bundle lets you pick up and move the starting fence, harvested crops can be replanted, and planting has its own sound.
- Watch the weather change, meet hungry worms, and teach your Mark III to practise stomping them overnight.
- New crow visits and bedtime scenes bring more life to your farm.

## Fixed

- **One touch is one tap.** On a tablet, the first tap after a pause could count twice, so planting a square also watered it. It now counts once.
- **A building that does not fit now says so.** When you put down a tower, coop or barn where it does not fit, the ground buzzes and the building's outline shows for a moment, with a red mark on each square in the way. Before, the tap tilled the ground.
- **Buildings no longer go on top of crops, eggs or another building.** The barn, coop, tower, robot stall and workbench refuse a spot with a crop in it, with the same buzz and outline. Sprinklers and robots can still stand among crops.
- **Robots walk round buildings** instead of through them, and leave their stall by its open front.
- **The worm stays in one piece** as it crawls, instead of coming apart at every bend.
- **"Pick up" walks the farmer to the machine first.** Choosing it for a sprinkler or robot used to lift it from across the farm.
- **A hen can get into her coop** when its door has moved to the side because the front is blocked.
- **Move buildings without losing what is inside.** Pick up a coop, tower, barn, robot stall or workbench and put it down elsewhere. Animals come outside, eggs and finished cheese tumble onto the grass to collect, and milk still being made into cheese travels with the barn.
- **Moved robots keep their settings.** A Mark I keeps its taught round, a Mark II keeps its job, and a Mark I kept in its crate overnight can work again the next day.
- **A game closed in the middle of saving no longer leaves an empty save file.**
