# v0.2.1 release-note evidence

This record checks the candidate release notes against `v0.2.0.1..origin/main`. It was first
written against `b7d07f9` (2026-10-08) and updated on 2026-10-12. It is not player-facing copy.

## Barn finding

*Updated 2026-10-12.* The first version of this record (against `b7d07f9`, 2026-10-08) found the
barn unreachable and told the notes to leave it out. That is no longer true, and Daniel ruled on
2026-10-12 that the cows and the creamery ship as they are. The notes' "New: the Industrial
Barn" section describes it. What a player can reach on `origin/main` now:

- The shop sells the barn after 10 eggs, with its first cow, and sells more cows
  (`1fc2a7e`, `ed7b447`, `8003ab2`, `4c3741e`, `49fca5d`).
- A tap opens the barn's panel, and Go inside enters the room (`b6aa791`; the barn route is in
  `systems/action_router.gd`).
- Cows walk in, give milk and walk out; the cheese line runs and its finished cheese sells from
  the shelf for 20 gold a batch (`71408bd`, `2c011ff`, `fb4f093`).
- The cow is black and white and bigger (`647d526`, `2727678`); cows graze and walk round
  beds and walls (`8dd7afc`, `d11541b`).
- The room is 12 by 8 squares with four pens and a large machine for each cheese station
  (`2d36466`).

Shipping the barn also pulled in changes to buildings and robots that players will notice:
no building goes on a crop (`d162e6e`), and robots walk round buildings (`2e4680a`, `df01f4b`).
