# The creamery: script v1 (about 30 seconds)

Short lines, so ad-libbing is easy. Every claim is checked against the game on main (2026-10-10).

# Hook

What's up TikTok? It's your boy Buddy-O Vlogs. I'm making a game for my four-year-old daughter, and every day I add a feature from your comments.

# Today's feature

Today's feature: the creamery.

# What it does

You put a big red barn on your farm. Your cows walk in on their own, whenever they're ready, and give milk.

The milk runs through steel pipes into a cheese line that makes cheese all by itself. It warms it, cuts it, stirs it, drains it, presses it.

Then one tap sells all your cheese.

# Design choices

Nobody herds the cows. They choose when to come in. And the barn is bigger on the inside than the outside.

# Ask

So what should I build tomorrow? Comment the next feature.

## What a longer cut (45–60 s) would add, all true today

- **It's a reward.** The barn unlocks after you've collected 10 eggs. It costs 500 gold with its first cow;
  more cows are 250 each, up to four in a barn.
- **Made for a four-year-old.** Each of the seven cheese machines shows its work for five seconds, so she can
  follow milk turning into curds, then slabs, then wheels, by colour and shape, without reading a word.
- **No milk meter.** How much milk a cow has is hidden on purpose. You learn she's ready by watching her
  walk into a stall.
- **Happy cows only.** My rule: open stalls, no gates, no ropes, and a cow can always walk back out.
- **It keeps working while you farm.** Four cows can give milk at once; their milk waits its turn and the line
  carries on without you. Each wheel sells for 20 gold.
