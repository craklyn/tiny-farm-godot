# Hook

What's up TikTok? It's your boy Buddy-O Vlogs. I'm making a game for my four-year-old daughter.

# Features

So far it's a farming game, obviously inspired by Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley. You can plant crops, you can water the crops, you can collect eggs from your single chicken. You have a chicken coop, you have a tower you can climb up, and you have AI bots that can walk your garden, water according to a pre-programmed order, or they can learn with reward mechanism how to feed your crops better.
