# The sheep: script v1 (about 30 seconds)

Short lines, so ad-libbing is easy. Every claim is checked against the game on main (e9a5fc3a, 2026-10-14).

You play it straight: a proud developer showing off a feature he thinks is finished. The audience should
want to shout "just add shears!" at every line. You never say "shear" (not even the coat name "freshly
sheared"), and nothing you say hints at a way to take the wool off.

# Hook

What's up TikTok? It's your boy Buddyo Vlogs. I'm making a game for my four-year-old daughter, and I add features from your comments.

# You asked

You asked: are there sheep?

Now there are!

# The wool

Every night, their wool grows. Full coat. Extra full coat.

At extra full, they squeeze through doors at quarter speed.

And at the extreme coat, she can't take another step. And she loves it.

# Things to do

It felt like there wasn't much for them to do after that. So I gave them things to do.

They graze a little bald spot. They stretch for the grass. And birds come sit on them.

# Ask

So what should I build next?

## What a longer cut (45–60 s) would add, all true today

- **Bought like a cow.** A sheep is 250 gold from the shop, on the card right beside the cow's, once you have
  the barn. She arrives with a mid-growth coat, so she's stuck for good after her third night.
- **They live in the barn.** Sheep rest in the barn's stalls between grazing. Cows and sheep share a barn's
  four places, so every sheep is one less cow. Four sheep, and the cow card goes dark: no more cows.
- **No milk.** A sheep never gives milk and never feeds the cheese line.
- **If she gets too fluffy while she's resting in a stall, that stall is hers forever.**
- **The wool isn't an item.** You can't collect it and you can't sell it. You just have the wool.
- **The bald spot takes a day.** On her first day stuck she grazes her own square and the eight round it, one
  every 25 seconds. After one night it's bare earth.
- **The grass she stretches for is real**: the square two out on the side she faces, drawn a little taller.
- **The birds are songbirds**, not the crows. They sit 12 to 22 seconds, as if she were a cloud. Every sheep
  looks a little different: eight looks.
